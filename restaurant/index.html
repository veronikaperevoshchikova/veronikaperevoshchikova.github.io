ABOUT US

Flavour is a gastrobar on Upper Lake where people come together around gastronomy, wine, and new flavors. Here you can experience signature cuisine, wine culture, and a unique atmosphere—it's a place for pleasant evenings in intimate settings. Flavour hosts unique gastronomic events: a wine club, chef's tables, and gala dinners. The menu is regularly updated to discover new flavors with each visit.

Our ideology

RUSSIAN TASTES IN EUROPEAN TRADITIONS

This menu is about warmth that can't always be explained in words. About care hidden in the details. And about a taste that stays with you longer than expected. I work with local products from our region and communicate Russian traditions in the modern language of European techniques.

Chef Oleg Razuvaev and Denis Kaplyuk
